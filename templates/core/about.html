🏠 Our Story

We're Building Nepal's Most Trusted Room Marketplace

RoomFinder Nepal was founded in 2026 after our co-founders spent weeks searching for a room in Kathmandu — encountering fake listings, ghost landlords, and deposit scams along the way. We decided to fix it.

Our Mission

A rental market built on trust, not luck

Every listing on RoomFinder goes through document verification — citizenship, Lalpurja, and identity checks — before it ever reaches a tenant's search results. We believe finding a room shouldn't mean gambling your deposit on a stranger's word.

What We Stand For

Verified, Always

Every landlord and listing is manually reviewed before it goes live — no exceptions.

Fast & Transparent

No hidden fees, no surprise charges — just clear pricing and honest listings.

For Everyone

Built for Nepal's renters and landlords alike, in every province — not just the capital.
